### 🛰️ About me

Computer Science student at the **Aristotle University of Thessaloniki**, working where **deep learning**, **robotics** and **space** meet: building models that perceive the world, make decisions and act on them, and pointing them at the sky whenever I can.

- 🧠 **Deep learning** · computer vision, generative models, reinforcement learning
- 🤖 **Robotics** · perception and learning-based control for autonomous systems
- 🌌 **Space** · AI for astronomical data and space exploration
